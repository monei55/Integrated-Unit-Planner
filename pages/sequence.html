Step 5

Teaching & Learning Sequence

Work backwards from the curriculum and assessment evidence to identify what students need to know, understand and do, then map the teaching, practice and application across the unit.

Backward planning principle: Students should not first encounter the knowledge, vocabulary, process or cognitive demand in the assessment. Use this section to make sure the required learning is explicitly taught, modelled, practised and applied before students are expected to demonstrate it independently.
1

Backward Planning Summary

What must students know, understand and be able to do to successfully demonstrate the selected curriculum and assessment?

K

KNOW

Facts, terminology, background knowledge and content students need to retrieve.

U

UNDERSTAND

Concepts, relationships, patterns, principles and important understandings students need to make sense of.

D

DO

Processes, skills and cognitive demands students must learn to perform independently.

2

Assessment Readiness

Check what needs to be explicitly taught and practised before each assessment component.

Build the year-level assessment in Step 4, then use Build readiness check.
3

Teaching Priorities

Identify the learning that requires explicit instruction, modelling, guided practice and independent application.

4

Integrated Weekly Learning Sequence

Build the actual teaching sequence using the weekly lesson allocations established in Unit Setup.

Integration principle: Selected learning areas do not need a separate lesson every time. HASS or Science knowledge can be deliberately developed through English reading, vocabulary, fluency and writing while retaining the dedicated disciplinary lesson.
5

Teaching Coverage Check

Check that important curriculum and assessment demands have been deliberately taught and practised before assessment.

Build the learning sequence first, then run the teaching coverage check.
