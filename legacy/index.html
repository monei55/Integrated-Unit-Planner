Integrated weekly learning sequence

Each week contains the actual subject lessons, a linked fluency passage plan, Daily Review focus and Learning Wall/Bump-It-Up updates. Suggestions are editable and movable.

Integration principle: selected subjects do not need to appear in every lesson. HASS/Science knowledge can be deliberately developed through English reading, vocabulary, writing and fluency while retaining the dedicated disciplinary lesson.
